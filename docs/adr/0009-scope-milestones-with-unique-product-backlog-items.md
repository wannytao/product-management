# Scope Delivery Milestones with unique Product Backlog Items

Every Delivery Milestone will contain one or more Strategic Product Backlog Items from its own Initiative, and a Product Backlog Item may belong to at most one active Milestone Scope. Users see the Milestone rather than its internal work breakdown, while the Product Owner retains a verifiable connection to delivery work; forbidding simultaneous active reuse prevents the same work from supporting multiple commitments and inflating delivery progress.
